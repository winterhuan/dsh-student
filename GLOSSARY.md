# Student learning

English | [中文](GLOSSARY.zh.md)

Student organizes a learner's study, parent-confirmed material and practice records. These terms distinguish learning activities, published exercises and the learner's own responses.

## Language

**Term**: The period of learning records maintained for one learner, covering their profile, courses, practice and progress.

_Avoid_: Learning block, DSH Session

**Learning block**: A continuous learning activity with a subject, mode and selected scope, which can include several questions. It is distinct from the DSH Session that carries the conversation.

_Avoid_: Session, conversation

**Learning goal**: A specific piece of knowledge or ability represented in a subject's knowledge map, including a confirmed concept in that subject.

_Avoid_: Free-text topic, textbook edition

**Worksheet**: A set of arranged questions prepared for paper practice, with the question content and answer basis.

_Avoid_: Transcription draft, assessment

**Learner response**: The answer the learner gives to a particular question. The question, its answer basis and subsequent feedback are separate from this response.

_Avoid_: Reference answer, model feedback

**Assessment**: The result and feedback for a learner response. Reassessing that same response is a correction, rather than another attempt.

_Avoid_: Learner response, new attempt

**Transcription draft**: Photo-derived question and response text, with proposed interpretations, awaiting parent review.

_Avoid_: Confirmed result, worksheet

**Assistance**: The help the learner uses while answering a question, distinct from whether the answer is correct or the transcription is reliable.

_Avoid_: Transcription confidence, answer correctness

**Answer date**: The calendar day on which the learner actually answers, distinct from the dates of receipt, assessment and correction.

_Avoid_: Receipt date, assessment date

**First mastery**: A learning goal first reaches mastery through learner-response evidence within the current term.

_Avoid_: Repeated practice, recovered mastery

**Recovered mastery**: A previously mastered goal returns to mastery through new learner responses after becoming weak or due for review.

_Avoid_: First mastery, evidence ageing out

**Completed review**: The learner answers a due mistake independently and correctly after it becomes due.

_Avoid_: Attempted review, correctness with help
